# Keld Code of Conduct

Keld welcomes people of every background and level of experience. Participation
should be safe, respectful and focused on building a useful project. This policy
applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions and other official Keld
spaces, and when someone represents Keld publicly.

## How we work together

Challenge claims, code and decisions with evidence. Criticise the work without
attacking the person. Explain problems clearly, make room for questions, credit
others' contributions, and respect decisions about someone's availability or
participation. An ambitious technical goal does not excuse mistreatment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, targeted humiliation,
publishing private information without consent, and retaliation against a
reporter or witness are unacceptable. Repeated unwanted contact, impersonation,
and deliberately disruptive behavior are also unacceptable. These standards
apply to maintainers as well as contributors.

## Report a concern privately

Email [hello@gyldlab.com](mailto:hello@gyldlab.com) with the subject
**Keld conduct report**. This is GYLDLAB's published contact address.
Include links, relevant context and the outcome or immediate assistance you need.
Share only information needed to understand the concern; do not post private
incident details in a public issue.

For reports that do not concern [@0monish](https://github.com/0monish), @0monish
coordinates with an uninvolved member of the [maintainer team](MAINTAINERS.md).
If a report concerns @0monish or another recipient of the shared organization
inbox, **do not send its details to that inbox**. For GitHub behavior, use the
affected content's **Report content → Report abuse to GitHub Support** option;
this private channel is outside the Keld maintainers' access. GitHub handles its
own platform rules, rather than promising a Keld investigation.

For an internal Keld investigation of a conflict case, first ask an uninvolved
listed maintainer for a separately controlled private contact, without sharing
incident details in public or with the named person. Share the report only after
that route is established. The named person is excluded from intake,
coordination, evidence access and decisions. If no independent route is available,
do not represent the shared inbox as a safe substitute; use the platform's
private reporting channel for behavior within its scope.

Maintainers handle reports discreetly and limit sharing to what is needed for
assessment and action. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality when safety,
platform rules or legal obligations require escalation. Retaliation is itself a
violation. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public
conduct discussions.

## Fair enforcement

An uninvolved maintainer reviews the available context, offers affected people a
reasonable opportunity to explain when appropriate, and records the rationale.
Maintainers may remove harmful content or limit interaction while investigating.
Depending on severity, repetition and safety needs, action can include a request
to stop, a warning, temporary restrictions or removal from project spaces.
Urgent safety concerns may require immediate restrictions.

Where safe, we explain the decision and its scope privately to the people
involved. You may request reconsideration through the same email channel; an
uninvolved maintainer reviews any new evidence. We aim to acknowledge reports
within three business days and provide an initial update within seven business
days. These are volunteer response goals, not guaranteed deadlines.
