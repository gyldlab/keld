## Reuse points

- `keld_guard::parse_manifest` / `load_manifest` (64 KiB, JSONC, unique-key) is the ONLY manifest decoder; migrate's generator must emit bytes that round-trip through it and must not add a second JSONC writer/parser (arch 03 §2 'KEL-102 … must reuse this parser').
- `keld_guard::DenyReason` fix text already prints `Add a grant at <json_pointer> in keld.permissions.jsonc` / `Append "<requested>" to …` — the migrate report's permission fix-its must reuse this wording owner (and `json_pointer_for`), not a parallel template.
- `DoctorFinding` + `KeldErrorObject{code,message,cause?,fix,docs}` (crates/keld-cli/src/doctor.rs, error_object.rs) is the structured finding shape for `keld migrate --json` and the future `keld_migrate_analyze` MCP tool (arch 07 §4 says it is read-only).
- `ReservedVerb::Migrate` / `KELD-CLI-045` (verb.rs) is the entry point to replace; its `tracking()` currently names KEL-17 and its fix text must change in the same PR the verb goes live (code/spec drift rule).
- `keld_compat::evidence` (KEL-74) is the sole scorer; any 'score' printed by migrate or the corpus harness must come from `score()` against a parsed denominator or not be a percentage at all.
- KEL-237 lifecycle corpus manifest (`corpus_id, scope, panel, kind, upstream{electron_version, electron_commit, app_docs}, cells[{operation_id, oracle_id, expected_verdict, test_path, test_name, negative_control, intentional_divergence?}]`) + `lifecycle_corpus.rs` runner (deny_unknown_fields, exact-bytes digest) is the corpus-manifest format to extend for app-level corpus entries, not fork.
- `packages/@keld/electron` tsconfig `paths` alias (and the bunfig.toml written for spec parity) is the live dev alias mechanism (arch 04 §3 v0 note); the scratchpad alias-probe shows Bun 1.4.2 also remaps `require('electron')` inside node_modules deps through tsconfig paths.
- @keld/api `app` lifecycle, `channels`, `isCallError`/`KeldCallError` (typed KELD-IPC-* errors) are what generated `keld.config.ts`/`keld.compat.ts` ultimately boot; migrate must not generate a second lifecycle owner.
- Scratchpad `scan_electron_usage.py` + `electron-api.json` (180 entities / 2128 members) + `compat-matrix.tsv` (2128 rows with family/tier/keld_status/demand columns) is the prototype of the demand scan and classification table; the committed matrix must have one owner (proposed keld-compat data file) consumed by analyzer, scoreboard and conformance alike.
- KEL-215 evidence rows (arch 04 §5) are the only legal native-module statements; `keld doctor` addon guidance 'will report only curated, version-qualified evidence rather than promising that a prebuild loads'.
- Arch 03 §2 manifest sketch already has the grant shapes migrate needs to target: `app.fs.read/write` with `$APPDATA/**`, `app.net.connect` URL origins, `app.shell.open` URL scopes, `app.shell.spawn [{cmd:"$RESOURCES/bin/ffmpeg", args:"reviewed"}]`, `app.system` tokens (clipboard.read/write, notifications, tray, global-shortcuts), `windows.<name>.channels` + `web.csp`.
- keld-update full-package activation + KEL-254 ExpectedAppIdentity are the targets for electron-builder `publish`/`asar`/fuses `OnlyLoadAppFromAsar`/`EnableEmbeddedAsarIntegrityValidation` concepts — do not invent a Keld fuse system.
- Research 186 NEW gaps 1-7 and research 214 Tables 3-5 (conditional oracle pin, ratchet, default-deny classification) define the test-identity and manifest-bytes rules the corpus harness must satisfy.
